Site Name

IS | Isadora Paula

IS | Isadora Paula is my personal creative brand, bringing together photography, filmmaking, graphic design, social media management, and creative content production. The website will strengthen my professional identity while showcasing my portfolio and services.

Why this name?
I chose this name because I want it to represent my journey, my dedication, and the legacy I hope to build through my creative work.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase my creative work, present the services I offer, and provide a professional portfolio where potential clients can learn about my experience, view previous projects, and request quotes.

Scenarios

  • How can I see examples of photography, video, and design projects created by IS | Isadora Paula?
  • How can I request a quote or contact Isadora Paula for a future project?

Website Structure

  • Home (index.html) – Brand presentation and featured work.
  • Services (services.html) – Photography, video, design, and digital services.
  • Portfolio (portfolio.html) – Gallery of completed projects with category filters.
  • Contact (contact.html) – Contact form and social media links.
